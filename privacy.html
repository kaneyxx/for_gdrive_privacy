Privacy policy for rclone-metasight

rclone-metasight is a personal OAuth client used only by the owner of github.com/kaneyxx to copy his own files between his computers, a cloud workspace, and his own Google Drive with rclone.

It requests only the Google Drive scope drive.file, which limits access to files created by this client. It does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data, and it is not offered to other users.

Contact: github.com/kaneyxx
